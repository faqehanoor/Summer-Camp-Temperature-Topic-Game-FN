# 🧑‍🍳 Chef's Recipe Chaos

> **Master the AI Temperature Dial — Too low = boring, too high = chaos, just right = creative genius!**

Chef's Recipe Chaos is an interactive educational web game that teaches one of the most important AI concepts: **Temperature in Large Language Models (LLMs)**.

Instead of learning through theory, players become a chef and discover how changing the AI's "temperature" affects creativity while cooking recipes for different customers.

---

## 🎯 AI Concept

This project teaches the **Temperature** concept from the **AI Prompting in 2026** course.

| Temperature  | AI Behavior         | Game Result      |
| ------------ | ------------------- | ---------------- |
| ❄️ 0.0 – 0.3 | Predictable         | Bland Recipes    |
| ✨ 0.4 – 0.7  | Creative & Balanced | Perfect Meals    |
| 🔥 0.8 – 1.0 | Random & Chaotic    | Disaster Recipes |

The goal is to understand that AI creativity depends on choosing the right temperature.

---

# 🎮 Gameplay

Players become a young chef who serves different customers.

Each customer wants a specific cooking style.

The player must:

* 🌡️ Adjust the AI Temperature slider
* 🍳 Cook the recipe
* ⭐ Earn stars
* 💰 Collect coins
* 🏆 Unlock achievements
* 👨‍🍳 Become the Master Chef

# 🎮 How to Play

1. Click **Start Cooking**
2. Read your customer's request.
3. Move the Temperature Slider.
4. Press **Cook**.
5. Match the customer's preferred temperature.
6. Earn Stars and Coins.
7. Unlock achievements.
8. Complete all rounds.
